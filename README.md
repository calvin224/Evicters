# Evicters

**Evicters** is a chaotic cooperative physics game where you and your crew work as professional eviction contractors.

Your job sounds simple: **get the occupants and their stuff out of the building.**

In reality, doors fight back, furniture gets stuck, objects go flying, and your carefully planned eviction quickly turns into absolute chaos.

> **Move fast. Break things. Get paid.**

## 🎮 About

Evicters is a **1–4 player cooperative comedy game** focused on physics-driven gameplay, teamwork, and emergent chaos.

Players take on the role of eviction contractors sent into properties to clear them out. Work together to move furniture, carry objects, knock down obstacles, and get everything outside before the job goes sideways.

The game is currently an **early prototype** and is being developed in Unity.

## ✨ Features

* 👥 **1–4 player cooperative gameplay**
* 💪 Physics-driven interactions
* 🛋️ Pick up, carry, throw and move objects

## 🕹️ Gameplay

Each job starts with a relatively straightforward objective:

**Clear the property.**

Players need to work together to:

1. Enter the property
2. Identify what needs to be removed
3. Move furniture and other objects
4. Deal with obstacles
5. Get everything out of the building
6. Complete the eviction
7. Get paid

The challenge comes from **how** you accomplish those objectives.

One player might carry a sofa through the front door while another holds the door open. Someone else might decide the sofa fits better through a window.

It probably doesn't.

## 🏗️ Project Structure

The project follows a component-based approach built around Unity's architecture.

Core gameplay systems include:

* `PlayerInteraction` — handles player interactions with the environment
* `Interactable` — base functionality for interactive objects
* `PickupObject` — handles physics-based object pickup and manipulation
* Door and environment interaction systems

The goal is to keep gameplay systems modular and reusable as the project grows.
